Our Story

Born from a love of real food.

ChopHouse started in 2018 as a small kitchen in Lagos, founded by Chef Adaeze Onyeka who grew up watching her grandmother cook over open fire. What began as a weekends-only supper club quickly became the neighbourhood's most-loved gathering spot.

Today we serve hundreds of guests daily, but our commitment has never changed: every pot is seasoned with patience, every meal is cooked to order, and every guest leaves full — in every sense of the word.
